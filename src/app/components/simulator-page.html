How to use the simulator

It starts from your predicted splits for the division above{{ formatNote() }}. Nothing here changes your profile or your prediction; it's a scratchpad for race plans and "what ifs".

  1. Change one split: drag its slider or type a time, e.g. see what a 30 s faster sled push is worth.
  2. Shift everything: move All runs or All stations by a percentage (negative = faster).
  3. Work back from a goal: type a target finish and tap Hit target to see the splits you'd need.
