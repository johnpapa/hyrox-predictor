How the prediction works

1 · Your 5K sets the baseline

Running is about half of a HYROX race, and in results data run total alone explains roughly 82% of the variation in finish time (r ≈ 0.91). Physiology research agrees: VO₂max is the strongest single correlate of finish time. We convert your 5K pace into a HYROX kilometre pace. Mid-pack athletes run about 20% slower than 5K pace, strong hybrid athletes about 12–15% slower, and first-timers up to 30% slower. Experience, training volume and Pro loads adjust this "compromised running" factor. The eight runs follow the real pacing shape: Run 1 is fastest, the runs after the sled push and the lunges are slowest, and Run 8 includes the finish straight.

2 · Stations start from real race medians

Station, run and Roxzone times come from median splits of about 15,000 Open results from results.hyrox.com, grouped by finish band. For your projected run pace we interpolate what athletes who run like you typically post on each station. We then adjust each station for you:

  • SkiErg / Row: your fresh 1000 m erg time × 1.13, since in the race you hold about 85–90% of time-trial pace. A 2 km row is converted to a 1 km equivalent. If you know only one erg time, it also informs the other.
  • Sled Push: uses your leg capacity (bodyweight + back squat) relative to a typical athlete. Sled Pull uses bodyweight + deadlift, plus grip.
  • Burpee Broad Jumps: aerobic level plus bodyweight. Heavier athletes are slower.
  • Farmers Carry: deadlift and grip. Sandbag Lunges: load relative to squat, plus leg endurance.
  • Wall Balls: your max unbroken set compared with the typical set for your level (about 100 for sub-60 athletes, 40 at 80–90 min, 20 at 120+).
  • Pro and mixed loads: heavier sleds, bells, bags and balls scale times using elasticities fitted to Pro vs Open splits at the same run pace.
  • Roxzone: typically 6–9% of race time. It is adjusted for experience and your self-rated transition speed.

3 · Missing data? Every ability has fallbacks

You can leave any field blank. Each ability uses the best information available, in this order:

  • Running: any race times you know (5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon), converted with the Riegel formula and blended: 10K and half marathon count most because they match a HYROX effort (60–120 min near threshold), the 5K slightly less, the marathon least. A 5K plus a longer race also adjusts for endurance. No race? VO₂max (lab or watch, via Daniels VDOT) › resting heart rate + age › self-rating.
  • Ergs: 1000 m time › 500 m, 2000 m or 5000 m (Paul's law: +5 s/500 m per doubling) › the other erg ± the typical SkiErg-vs-row gap › self-rating.
  • Leg strength: back squat (any reps; 1RM via Epley) › front squat ÷ 0.85 › deadlift × 0.8 › trap-bar or Romanian deadlift › leg press or bench (rough) › self-rating mapped to strength standards (Weak 0.75× … Elite 2.75× bodyweight for men).
  • Pulling strength: deadlift › trap bar ÷ 1.08 › Romanian deadlift ÷ 0.75 › squat ÷ 0.8 › bench (rough) › self-rating.
  • Grip: farmers carry test › dead hang and/or pull-ups, placed on a level scale › self-rating.
  • Burpees: 80 m BBJ test › burpees in 1 minute › self-rating.
  • Wall balls: 100 reps for time › "Karen" (150 reps) × 0.62 › max unbroken › self-rating.
  • Sleds and lunges: fresh station test × race-fatigue factor › strength and bodyweight model, adjusted by a technique self-rating.

Every self-rating has a concrete description for each level, so "Strong" means the same thing to everyone. "Not sure" assumes a typical athlete at your running level, so it never skews the result; it only widens the confidence range. The confidence panel shows which inputs are measured, estimated, self-rated or assumed, and which single input would narrow your range most.

4 · Doubles & relay

Doubles: partners run together, so each run goes at the slower partner's pace, though it is less compromised because each of you does only part of the station work. Doing a share of a station lets you work at higher intensity. The model is calibrated so an even split matches the observed doubles-to-singles ratios: sleds about 0.55–0.6×, wall balls about 0.66×, ergs about 0.9×. "Auto all" finds the fastest split for each station. Relay: each athlete runs 2 × 1 km and does 2 stations on relatively fresh legs. The fastest athlete order is chosen automatically.

Accuracy

From a 5K alone, expect roughly ±10%. Each benchmark you add narrows the range, and a previous HYROX result narrows it most. Venues differ: sled surfaces and Roxzone lengths vary, so treat the result as a pacing plan, not a promise. The field position is approximate and based on historical finish distributions.

Sources: results.hyrox.com split data (via public scraped datasets), HyroxDataLab station and Roxzone analyses, Brandt et al. 2025 (Frontiers in Physiology) on HYROX physiological determinants, and published HYROX 25/26 rulebooks for weights.
